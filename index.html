What this call covers is laid out above — so before booking, it's on you to decide if you're ready to take that first step. If you're not sure yet, that's worth sitting with before you pay for the call.

Do you fund or cover the capital for my business? +

No — but we can help facilitate the path to get there, whether that's financing options, structuring your ask, or knowing what a lender or investor will want to see.

That's really up to you. We move as fast as your timeline calls for — the faster you want to go, the faster we build.

Do you guarantee my business will succeed? +

Nobody can guarantee that but you. Any business can succeed if the right time and effort is applied to it — what we guarantee is that you'll have what you need to give it that shot.

What happens after my business launches? +

You run it and manage it — it's yours. We're here if you want additional support after launch; ask about retainer plans on your call.
